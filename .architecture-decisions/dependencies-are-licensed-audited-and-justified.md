# Dependencies are licensed, audited and justified

Only permissive licenses are allowed, so StarlightPDF stays usable under Apache-2.0 in closed-source products; cargo-deny checks licenses and advisories. cargo-vet requires a source audit of every package version, recorded here or imported from Mozilla, Google, Bytecode Alliance or ISRG, and no exemptions. Each direct dependency's reason is a `#` comment on its manifest line.
